Privacy Policy

How Mastering NDA/NA English handles information used to operate the website and its learning services.

Effective date: 03 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Mastering NDA/NA English collects, uses and protects information when you use this website, create an account, attempt online practice tests or otherwise interact with our services.

1. Information you provide

When registration, login or profile features are used, we may collect information you provide, such as your name, email address and profile or class-related details. We also store information connected with your learning activity, such as quiz attempts, scores, accuracy, test duration and related progress information.

2. Automatically stored information

The website may use browser storage technologies such as local storage, session storage and cookies where necessary for login, preferences, test progress, security and site functionality. Standard technical information may also be processed by our hosting and service providers, such as browser type, device information, IP address, access time and requested pages.

3. How we use information

  • To provide registration, login, user dashboard and practice-test functionality.
  • To save and display test attempts, scores and learning progress.
  • To maintain site security, diagnose technical problems and improve usability.
  • To respond to queries or support requests.
  • To measure site performance and, where enabled, support advertising and analytics.

4. Supabase and service providers

Parts of the website use third-party infrastructure and services, including Supabase, for features such as authentication, data storage and related backend functions. Information processed through these services is handled subject to the relevant provider's terms and privacy practices.

5. Google advertising, cookies and identifiers

The website may use Google AdSense or other Google advertising services. Where advertising is enabled, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar identifiers to serve, personalise, limit and measure advertisements, detect fraud and understand advertising performance. Users may be able to manage personalised advertising through Google's advertising settings and other consent controls made available on the site or by Google.

Where required by applicable law, including for users in regions requiring consent for advertising cookies or personalised ads, an appropriate consent-management mechanism may be presented before such technologies are used.

6. Third-party links

The website may contain links to external websites, official examination resources, marketplaces, videos or other third-party services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites.

7. Data retention and security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, subject to operational, legal and security requirements. Reasonable safeguards are used to protect data, but no internet-based system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your choices

You may choose not to provide optional information. Browser settings can be used to control cookies and local storage, although disabling required storage may affect login, dashboard or test functionality. Where advertising consent controls are available, you may use them to manage your advertising preferences.

9. Children's privacy

This website provides educational material that may be used by school students. Users should provide only the information reasonably necessary to use the educational features. Where parental or guardian consent is required by applicable law, users and guardians are responsible for complying with those requirements.

10. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when website features, service providers, legal requirements or advertising arrangements change. The effective date shown above will be updated when material revisions are made.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please use the Author & Contact page.

This policy describes the website's current and planned operating practices. Advertising disclosures apply when the relevant advertising services are enabled.

NDA English Syllabus for GAT

The English component of the General Ability Test checks whether a candidate can understand and use English accurately in exam conditions. Use this page as a focused study map, then practise each area through the linked resources.

Reviewed: September 2026 · Independent educational guidance · Always check the latest UPSC notification

What UPSC specifies for NDA English

In the NDA/NA scheme, English forms Part A of the General Ability Test. The notified syllabus focuses on grammar and usage, vocabulary, comprehension and cohesion in extended text. In preparation terms, that means you should combine rule-based accuracy with reading, contextual vocabulary and sentence-level understanding.

Core areas to prepare

Grammar & UsageSubject–verb agreement, tenses, articles, prepositions, pronouns, modifiers, conjunctions, voice, narration and error spotting.
VocabularySynonyms, antonyms, idioms, one-word substitutions, phrasal verbs, confusing words and contextual meaning.
ComprehensionMain idea, inference, factual understanding, tone, contextual meaning and logical reading.
Cohesion & Sentence SenseOrdering, connectors, sentence relationships and choosing language that makes a passage coherent.

How to convert the syllabus into a study plan

  1. Learn or revise one grammar/vocabulary area at a time.
  2. Solve topic-wise PYQs immediately after revision.
  3. Maintain an error notebook for rules and words you repeatedly miss.
  4. Shift to year-wise PYQs to understand mixed-paper difficulty.
  5. Use timed sample papers to build decision speed and accuracy.

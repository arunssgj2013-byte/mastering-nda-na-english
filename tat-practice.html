SSB-Style TAT Practice

This practice follows the requested format: 11 pictures and a 12th blank slide. Observe each for 30 seconds; then the image disappears and you have 4 minutes to write a story.

How it works: Observe for 30 seconds → write for 4 minutes → automatically move to the next picture. Eleven pictures are followed by one blank slide.

Read the complete TAT instructions
  1. Observe each picture carefully for 30 seconds, without writing during the observation period.
  2. After the picture disappears, write a story within 4 minutes.
  3. Explain what led to the scene, what is happening, and what happens afterwards.
  4. Give your story a beginning, a central development and an ending.
  5. Use imagination, but keep events, decisions and solutions practical and realistic.
  6. Choose familiar human situations rather than fairy tales or borrowed stories.
  7. Where useful, include characters' names, approximate ages, thoughts, feelings and actions.
  8. Do not worry about decorative language; write naturally and clearly.
  9. Avoid undesirable or harmful conduct; let characters respond responsibly and realistically.
  10. Some scenes are intentionally vague. Interpret them independently.
  11. The twelfth slide is blank: imagine your own situation for 30 seconds, then write a story in 4 minutes.

Remember: Imagination · Practicality · No undesirable activity · A realistic, positive or happy ending. Let the outcome follow naturally from the characters’ actions.

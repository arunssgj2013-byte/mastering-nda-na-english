Original PPDT practice image

The picture is now hidden. Record your first perception without reopening it.

WRITE THESE DETAILS ON YOUR PPDT SHEET
  1. Number of characters: how many people you perceived.
  2. Character details: approximate age, sex and mood for each person. Use M/F/P if that matches your sheet's instructions; P means person when sex is unclear. Use + / 0 / − for positive / neutral / negative mood if your sheet uses these symbols.
  3. First character noticed: circle that person's details on your sheet.
  4. Action: write a short title or one-line summary of what is happening.
  5. Setting: note the place or background if it helps you remember the scene.

Type your notes in the field below or write them by hand.

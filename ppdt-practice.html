Practise the PPDT sequence

Observe the scene, note what you perceive, and write a short, constructive story. Each mock uses an original hazy picture. Your story is your own; this page gives no model answer.

30 secPicture observation
1 minCharacter and action notes

Timing note: Many PPDT practice guides use 30 seconds to view the picture and 4 minutes to write. Some guides include a separate 1-minute character/action noting stage. This practice centre offers that extra stage; follow the exact instructions given by your SSB board.

How to use each attempt

During the picture stage, observe the setting and people. After it disappears, record the main character's approximate age, mood and action. Then write a beginning, practical response and outcome in your own words.

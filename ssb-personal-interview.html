Preparation Tips & Common Mistakes

Approach your interview naturally

Know yourself, explain genuine responsibilities and personal growth, use clear language, stay respectful, and build confidence through real practice. Answers must agree with your PIQ.

Avoid these common mistakes

False claims, inconsistencies with PIQ details, memorised robotic answers, disrespectful remarks about family or friends, overconfidence, poor listening and unsupported guesses about defence or current affairs.

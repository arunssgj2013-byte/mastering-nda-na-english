Situation Reaction Test (SRT)

Stage II · Psychology Practice

Choose an SRT Practice Set

Each set contains 60 situations presented individually for 30 seconds each (30 minutes total). The next situation appears automatically. Write your reaction on paper.

Suggested reactions appear only after the test ends. They are examples, not official answers or a personality assessment. Sets 2–5 include new scenarios alongside additional constraints on earlier situations for extra practice.

Prepare pen and paper numbered 1–60. The test advances automatically, without a pause or back button. Avoid switching apps during practice.

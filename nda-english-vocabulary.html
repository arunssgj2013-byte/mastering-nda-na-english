Vocabulary for NDA English

NDA English Vocabulary Guide

Vocabulary grows through repeated exposure, context and revision. Focus on words that recur in competitive English and reinforce them with PYQs.

What to cover

Synonyms & AntonymsMeaning, shades of sense and option elimination.
Idioms & PhrasesCommon figurative expressions and their contextual use.
Phrasal VerbsVerb-particle combinations frequently tested in objective English.
Confusing WordsHomonyms, commonly confused pairs and spelling-sensitive vocabulary.

Revision method

  1. Learn words in small groups.
  2. Use meaning plus one short context.
  3. Revise at spaced intervals.
  4. Solve vocabulary PYQs.
  5. Keep a personal list of words you repeatedly miss.

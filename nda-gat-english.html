General Ability Test

NDA GAT English

English is Part A of the General Ability Test. It rewards accurate grammar, usable vocabulary, comprehension and the ability to recognise coherent English.

What GAT English tests

SyllabusGrammar and usage, vocabulary, comprehension and cohesion.Exam PatternUnderstand marks and the written-paper structure.PYQsStudy the level and recurring question styles.Mock PracticeBuild timing and decision-making under pressure.

Preparation sequence

  1. Know the syllabus.
  2. Revise grammar and vocabulary in parallel.
  3. Solve topic-wise PYQs.
  4. Move to complete year-wise English sets.
  5. Use timed mock tests before the examination.

Preparation Guide

Understanding the Five Descriptions

The prompts below reflect common SDT practice. Write naturally and give realistic observations; different people may see different aspects of your behaviour.

1. Parents' opinion

Consider responsibilities at home, habits, conduct and areas your parents encourage you to improve. Avoid inventing praise.

2. Teachers' or superiors' opinion

Think about punctuality, effort, learning attitude, teamwork, conduct and feedback you have actually received.

3. Friends' or colleagues' opinion

Reflect on reliability, cooperation, communication and how you respond to disagreements.

4. Your own opinion

Describe strengths, limitations, interests and examples from daily life without exaggeration.

5. Qualities you wish to improve

Identify genuine areas for growth and specific, achievable steps you are taking.

There is no single ideal SDT answer. Memorised templates may be less useful than thoughtful, truthful self-description.

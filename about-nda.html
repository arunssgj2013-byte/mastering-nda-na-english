NDA/NA Overview

What the examination is, why it is conducted and who conducts it.

The National Defence Academy and Naval Academy Examination (NDA/NA) is a competitive examination conducted by the Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) for admission to the Army, Navy and Air Force wings of the National Defence Academy and to the applicable 10+2 Cadet Entry Scheme at the Indian Naval Academy.

KEY POINTS AT A GLANCE
Purpose

Entry to officer training

The examination forms the written stage of selection for eligible young candidates seeking entry to the NDA/NA training routes notified by UPSC.

Conducting Body

Union Public Service Commission

UPSC conducts the written examination and publishes the official notification, question papers, results and examination-related updates.

Academy

Tri-service foundation

NDA provides joint training to cadets of the Army, Navy and Air Force before their subsequent service-specific training.

Frequency

NDA/NA I & II

UPSC normally schedules two NDA/NA examination cycles in a year, designated Examination (I) and Examination (II); exact dates are notification-specific.

02

Eligibility Criteria

Core eligibility conditions. Always verify the notification for the specific cycle.

Eligibility depends on nationality, date of birth, marital status, educational qualification and prescribed physical/medical standards. UPSC states the exact conditions in each examination notice.

ELIGIBILITY — POINT BY POINT
Sex & Marital Status

Unmarried male and female candidates

Eligible candidates must satisfy the marital-status conditions in the applicable UPSC notice and the training-related undertaking specified there.

Age / DOB

Use the notification's exact DOB window

UPSC specifies eligibility through exact permissible dates of birth for each examination. A fixed generic age range should not replace the current notification.

Army Wing

10+2 pass or equivalent

For the Army Wing of NDA: 12th Class pass of the 10+2 pattern of school education, or an equivalent examination, as prescribed by UPSC.

Air Force / Navy / INA

10+2 with Physics, Chemistry & Mathematics

For Air Force and Naval Wings of NDA and the 10+2 Cadet Entry Scheme at INA: 12th Class pass with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics, or equivalent.

Class XII Appearing

May apply subject to conditions

Candidates appearing in the 12th Class/equivalent examination may also apply, subject to documentary requirements and deadlines in the notice.

Nationality & Fitness

Notification conditions apply

Nationality/certificate-of-eligibility provisions and the prescribed physical and medical standards must also be satisfied.

Important: The date-of-birth window and medical standards are examination/course specific. Candidates should verify these directly from the UPSC notification applicable to the examination they are applying for.
03

Exam Structure

The complete selection structure before looking at individual written-paper details.

The NDA/NA selection framework combines a 900-mark written examination with a 900-mark SSB Test/Interview. Final selection remains subject to eligibility, medical fitness, merit and service/course preference.

SELECTION MARKS AT A GLANCE
900Written Examination
900SSB Test / Interview
1800Combined Maximum
Written Papers

Objective type

Paper I is Mathematics and Paper II is the General Ability Test. UPSC specifies objective-type questions for the written papers.

Language

Maths & GAT Part B bilingual

The Mathematics paper and Part B (General Knowledge) of GAT are set bilingually in Hindi and English.

Qualifying Standard

Fixed by UPSC

UPSC has discretion to fix qualifying marks in any or all subjects of the written examination.

Calculator

Not permitted

Calculators and mathematical/logarithmic tables are not permitted for the objective-type papers.

NDA/NA Syllabus

The official syllabus is divided into Mathematics and the General Ability Test.

The written syllabus has two papers. The list below gives the official major areas in a clean preparation-friendly form; candidates should still refer to the detailed UPSC syllabus for the full scope of each topic.

SUBJECT-WISE SYLLABUS

Paper I — Mathematics

  • Algebra
  • Matrices and Determinants
  • Trigonometry
  • Analytical Geometry of Two and Three Dimensions
  • Differential Calculus
  • Integral Calculus and Differential Equations
  • Vector Algebra
  • Statistics and Probability

Paper II — General Ability Test

  • Part A — English
  • Grammar and Usage
  • Vocabulary
  • Comprehension and Cohesion in Extended Text
  • Part B — Physics
  • Chemistry and General Science
  • Social Studies and Geography
  • Current Events
GAT Part B indicative weightage: UPSC gives approximately 25% Physics, 15% Chemistry, 10% General Science, 20% Social Studies, 20% Geography and 10% Current Events out of the marks assigned to Part B.

Written Exam Pattern

Complete Mathematics + GAT paper pattern before the English-specific guidance.

The current NDA/NA written format contains two objective papers on the same examination day. Mathematics carries 300 marks and GAT carries 600 marks, with 2½ hours allowed for each paper.

Selection Process

The route from the UPSC written examination to final allocation/admission.

Qualifying the written examination is only the first major stage. Candidates then proceed through the Services Selection Board process and prescribed medical examination before final selection, subject to merit and other eligibility conditions.

SELECTION FLOW — STEP BY STEP
01UPSC Written Examination
02Written Qualification
03SSB Stage I & II
04Medical Examination
05Merit & Allocation
Final selection: UPSC rules provide for selection/allocation subject to eligibility, medical fitness, available vacancies, merit and the candidate's service/course preferences.
08

SSB & Medical

A concise orientation to the post-written selection stages.

The SSB procedure uses a two-stage selection process. Candidates who clear Stage I are permitted to proceed to Stage II. Medical fitness is assessed separately under the standards applicable to the service/course.

SSB & MEDICAL — STAGE-WISE OVERVIEW
SSB Stage I

OIR + PP&DT

Stage I comprises Officer Intelligence Rating (OIR) tests and the Picture Perception & Description Test (PP&DT). Shortlisting is based on combined performance.

SSB Stage II

Interview, GTO, Psychology & Conference

Stage II comprises the Interview, Group Testing Officer Tasks, Psychology Tests and Conference; UPSC states that these tests are conducted over four days.

Air Force Flying

CPSS where applicable

Candidates considered for the Air Force flying route must satisfy the Computerised Pilot Selection System (CPSS) requirements prescribed in the notification.

Medical Examination

Service-specific standards

Recommended candidates must satisfy the prescribed medical/physical standards. Detailed standards can change and should be checked in the applicable official notice.

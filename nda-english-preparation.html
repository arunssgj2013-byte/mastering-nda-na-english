Study Strategy

How to Prepare for NDA English

A strong plan is simple: know the syllabus, strengthen fundamentals, solve real questions, analyse errors and then practise under time pressure.

Phase 1 — Build the base

Revise grammar and vocabulary in manageable blocks. Attach objective questions to every topic rather than postponing practice.

Phase 2 — Use topic-wise and year-wise PYQs

PYQs show what actually gets tested. Attempt them before seeing answers, then classify mistakes and revise weak areas.

Phase 3 — Add timed practice

Use sample papers and online sets to improve speed, selection and review habits. Accuracy remains important because objective NDA papers use negative marking.

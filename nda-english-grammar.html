Grammar & Usage

NDA English Grammar Guide

Grammar preparation should focus on rule clarity, error recognition and repeated application through objective questions.

High-value grammar areas

Subject–Verb AgreementAgreement with complex subjects, either/neither, collective nouns and intervening phrases.
Tenses & Verb PatternsTime relationships, sequence, infinitives, gerunds and common verb constructions.
Articles & PrepositionsUsage, fixed combinations and common competitive-exam errors.
Voice, Narration & TagsTransformation-based questions and standard rule patterns.

A better grammar routine

  1. Revise one rule group.
  2. Solve 15–30 objective questions immediately.
  3. Write the rule behind each mistake.
  4. Re-attempt missed questions after several days.
  5. Use full PYQ sets to test mixed grammar application.

READING COMPREHENSION

NDA English Comprehension Guide

Build the reading skills needed to answer passage-based questions accurately and quickly. Focus on what the passage actually says, what it logically implies and how words behave in context.

Focused NDA/NA English preparation · Reviewed: September 2026

Skills to master

Main Idea & PurposeIdentify the central message, author’s purpose and the best summary of a passage.
Facts & DetailsLocate explicitly stated information without adding outside assumptions.
Inference & ToneDraw conclusions supported by the passage and recognise the writer’s attitude.
Contextual MeaningWork out the meaning of words and expressions from surrounding sentences.

How to attempt a passage

  1. Read the passage for structure and central idea before chasing individual details.
  2. Underline mentally the key contrast words, causes, results and opinions.
  3. For factual questions, return to the exact sentence that supports the answer.
  4. For inference questions, choose only what is strongly supported—not what merely sounds possible.
  5. For vocabulary in context, substitute the option back into the sentence and check the meaning.
  6. Practise under time limits so reading speed improves without sacrificing accuracy.

Practice pathway

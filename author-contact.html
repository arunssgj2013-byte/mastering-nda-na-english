About the Author

Arun Kumar

Author • Mentor • Guide

Mr. Arun Kumar, an alumnus of Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya, Barun (Aurangabad, Bihar), holds a Master’s degree in English from Patna University. With over 13 years of teaching experience at Sainik School Gopalganj, under the aegis of the Sainik Schools Society, Ministry of Defence, Government of India, he has mentored numerous NDA/NA aspirants and contributed significantly to their academic preparation.

His in-depth knowledge of English language instruction, combined with a thorough understanding of the exam pattern and evolving trends, positions him as a trusted mentor in the field of competitive exam preparation. He has been awarded the Letter of Appreciation twice by the MHRD, Government of India, in recognition of the stellar performance of his cadets in the CBSE Board Examinations.

His books are a culmination of classroom experience, exam-oriented strategies and sustained commitment to helping NDA/NA aspirants develop the accuracy, speed and confidence required to excel in the English section.

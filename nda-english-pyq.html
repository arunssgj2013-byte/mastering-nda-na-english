Best way to use PYQs

  1. Attempt a set without checking answers.
  2. Classify mistakes into grammar, vocabulary, comprehension and careless errors.
  3. Revise the weak area immediately.
  4. Re-attempt after a gap.
  5. Move to timed online practice once accuracy stabilises.

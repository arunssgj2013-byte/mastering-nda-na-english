Word Association Test (WAT)

Stage II · Psychology Practice

10 Timed WAT Practice Sets

Each set displays 60 words, one at a time for 15 seconds each. Write a spontaneous, meaningful sentence by hand for every word. The sequence advances automatically and cannot be paused.

Keep a pen and paper ready before starting. There is no typing box, answer key or psychological score. These are unofficial SSB-style practice sets; actual board instructions take precedence.

Choose a set

A word may appear in more than one set, but never repeats within the same set. The practice is designed for response fluency, not memorised sentences.

Ready?

Prepare numbered paper (1–60). The test lasts 15 minutes. Once started, it runs automatically even if you switch tabs; avoid leaving this page.

00:15

Write your sentence on paper. The next word appears automatically.

Practice complete

Keep your handwritten responses. Review whether they are clear, spontaneous, realistic and grammatically understandable. Do not assume that a single response indicates personality or selection suitability.

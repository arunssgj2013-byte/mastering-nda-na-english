WAT Guidelines & Tips — Read Before Practice

✓ Effective Strategies

  • Be natural and honest: Express your genuine thoughts, not memorised answers.
  • Practise regularly: Develop quick thinking and confidence.
  • Stay constructive: Show practical thinking without forcing positive ideas.
  • Be original: Avoid clichés and repeated expressions.
  • Write clearly: Use a short, relevant, meaningful sentence.
  • Build vocabulary: Understand words and express ideas precisely.
  • Manage time: Respond promptly and move to the next word.

! Mistakes to Avoid

  • Unnecessarily aggressive or hostile responses.
  • Making every sentence about yourself.
  • Repeatedly expressing fear or hopelessness.
  • Vague, unrelated or meaningless sentences.
  • Using the same idea or sentence pattern repeatedly.
  • Complicated wording meant only to impress.
  • Panicking when you miss a word; focus on the next one.

Remember: WAT is not about perfect English or manufactured answers. Remain calm, genuine and clear. Practice format: 60 words · 15 seconds each · 15 minutes total.

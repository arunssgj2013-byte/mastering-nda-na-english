NDA English Exam Pattern

English is Part A of the General Ability Test. This guide explains where English fits in the written examination and how to prepare for objective questions efficiently.

Written examination structure

PaperMarks
Mathematics300
General Ability Test (GAT)600
English within GAT200
General Knowledge within GAT400

The written examination is objective in nature and incorrect answers attract negative marking as specified by UPSC. Always check the current notification for exact instructions.

How to prepare for the English section

  1. Strengthen grammar and usage.
  2. Revise vocabulary systematically.
  3. Solve year-wise PYQs without seeing answers first.
  4. Use timed mock tests to improve speed and accuracy.

Situation

At 09:00, your group of eight volunteers is at the Central Junction. You have one jeep (driver plus four passengers), two bicycles, two basic first-aid kits and mobile phones. Roads permit an average of 30 km/h by jeep and 10 km/h by bicycle. Walking speed is 5 km/h. The clinic and police post are staffed. Do not assume additional vehicles or specialist rescue equipment.

Map distances: Central Junction to North Village 4 km; Central Junction to East Clinic 6 km; Central Junction to South Market 3 km; Central Junction to West Post 5 km. Routes between outer points pass through the junction.

Planning time: Individual written plan · 10:00

Your typed plan stays in this browser session only; it is not submitted or scored.

Illustrative Planning Guidance

No single answer is guaranteed. Discuss feasibility, safety, resource allocation and whether the team can reasonably accomplish its priorities.

    Time-distance reference

    At 30 km/h, 3 km by jeep takes about 6 minutes, 4 km about 8 minutes, 5 km about 10 minutes and 6 km about 12 minutes. At 10 km/h, 4 km by bicycle takes about 24 minutes. Allow additional time for reporting, assistance and delays.

    Self-review

    • Did you identify every problem and the immediate threats to life?
    • Did you assign only resources actually available?
    • Are your route, travel time and sequence realistic?
    • Did you involve emergency professionals where necessary?
    • Can the group explain and agree on one workable plan?

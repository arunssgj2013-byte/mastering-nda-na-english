SSB Response Assessor

Practice feedback for WAT · TAT · SRT · PPDT

Practice tool, not an SSB board assessment. It gives feedback on written responses and possible OLQ evidence. A written answer cannot establish a candidate’s overall personality or predict recommendation. For handwritten work, upload a clear photo or PDF scan. The AI transcribes it first and flags unclear reading.

Include each stimulus with its answer where possible. Several responses from one set may be submitted together.
Up to four JPG, PNG or WebP photos, or one PDF. Maximum 8 MB total. Up to five reviews per 24 hours. Keep pages upright, well lit and in focus.
OLQ-informed practice feedback · Educational use only · Responses are not saved by this page.
